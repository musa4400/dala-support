Dala: Privacy Policy

Traditional Sudanese Strategy Board Game
Effective Date: September 20, 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how Dala ("we," "us," or "our") collects, uses, stores, and protects information when you download, install, play, or interact with our mobile game Dala (the "Game") on iOS and Android platforms.

By downloading, installing, or playing Dala, you agree to the collection and use of information in accordance with this Privacy Policy. If you do not agree with this policy, please do not use the Game.

1. Information We Collect

A. Information You Provide Directly

Duelist Display Name / Nickname: When customizing your player profile, you may choose a display name. This nickname is stored locally on your device and transmitted solely to identify your board during multiplayer or friend room sessions. We do not require your real name, email, physical address, or phone number to play the Game.

B. Information Stored Locally on Your Device

To provide a persistent, smooth experience without requiring an account login, Dala saves gameplay progress directly on your device using local storage (PlayerPrefs):

  • Campaign Progression: Unlocked difficulty levels (Level 1 through Level 6 Master) and AI defeat records.
  • Player Statistics: Matches won/lost, win streaks, Dalas formed, captures made, and dominance rating.
  • In-Game Economy: Earned and spent Coins and coin balances.
  • Audio & Gameplay Preferences: Sound/music volumes, selected loading sound theme, move indicators, and battery saver mode.

You can wipe this data at any time using the "RESET PROGRESS" button in the in-game Settings menu or by deleting the app.

C. Information Collected Automatically via Third-Party SDKs

When you play Dala, third-party software development kits (SDKs) integrated into the game may automatically collect technical diagnostics and device information:

  • Device Information: Device model, operating system version, screen resolution, and system language.
  • Advertising Identifiers: Apple IDFA (subject to explicit App Tracking Transparency consent on iOS) or Google Advertising ID (GAID) on Android.
  • Performance Metrics: App launch events, crash reports, and frame rate metrics.

2. Third-Party Services & Advertising

We work with trusted third-party partners to provide monetization, advertising, and platform services:

A. Advertising Networks

We serve non-intrusive advertisements (such as optional rewarded video ads for daily coins and interstitial ads between matches) through:

  • Google AdMob: Collects device IDs, IP addresses, and ad interaction metrics to deliver ads and prevent fraud. Google AdMob Privacy Policy
  • Unity Ads: Serves in-game rewarded and interstitial video ads. Unity Ads Privacy Policy
Ad-Free Option: You can permanently remove all interstitial, banner, and cadence advertisements at any time by purchasing the "Remove Ads" pass inside the in-game Treasury or Settings menu.

B. In-App Purchases & Payments

Dala offers optional digital purchases (Coin bundles, Level 6 Master AI permanent unlock, and Remove Ads).

  • All payments are securely processed by the Apple App Store (via StoreKit) or Google Play Store (via Google Play Billing).
  • We do not collect, view, or store your credit card, debit card, or banking details.
  • Apple Privacy Policy | Google Privacy Policy

C. Online Multiplayer & Matchmaking

When you use the "Play with a Friend" room code or online matchmaking, temporary session tokens and board coordinates are transmitted in real-time to synchronize moves between players. We do not record private chats, voice audio, or sensitive user data.

3. How We Use Information

We use the collected information exclusively to:

  • Maintain your game progress, level unlocks, and player statistics.
  • Facilitate multiplayer matches and room-code sessions.
  • Verify and fulfill in-app purchases (Level 6 unlock, coins, Remove Ads).
  • Deliver non-intrusive advertisements and rewarded video rewards.
  • Diagnose crashes and optimize performance across devices.

4. Children’s Privacy (COPPA)

Dala is designed for general audiences and tactical board game players. We do not knowingly collect personal identifiable information from children under 13 (or under 16 in the European Economic Area / UK). If you believe a child has provided personal information to us, please contact us and we will promptly delete it.

5. Your Privacy Rights & Choices

Under regulations such as GDPR (EEA/UK) and CCPA/CPRA (California), you have the right to:

  • Delete Your Account & Data: You can permanently delete your cloud account, Cloud Save backup, linked Sign in with Apple / Google credentials, and all associated profile data directly inside the app via Settings > Options > DELETE ACCOUNT (or Duelist Profile > DELETE ACCOUNT). You can also reset local device progress via Settings > Options > RESET PROGRESS.
  • Opt-Out of Personalized Ads: On iOS, you can decline the App Tracking Transparency prompt or go to Settings > Privacy & Security > Tracking. On Android, go to Settings > Google > Ads to reset or delete your advertising ID.
  • Disable Ads: You can purchase the permanent Remove Ads VIP pass in-game.

6. Data Security

We implement standard safeguards to protect your game data. All financial transactions are secured directly by Apple and Google. While no wireless transmission is 100% secure, we take reasonable precautions to protect against unauthorized access.

7. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy periodically. Any modifications will be posted on this page with an updated Effective Date. Continued use of the Game constitutes acceptance of any updates.
