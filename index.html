Frequently Asked Questions

1. How do I restore my previous In-App Purchases (Remove Ads / Level 6 Oracle)?

Non-consumable purchases such as Remove Ads (Lifetime VIP) and Grandmaster Level 6 (The Oracle) are permanently tied to your Apple ID or Google Play account and can be restored on any device at no extra cost:

  1. Make sure you are signed in to the same Apple ID or Google Play account used for the original purchase.
  2. Open Dala and tap RESTORE PURCHASES inside Pharaoh's Treasury, the AI Sacred Trials screen, the Coin Store, or Settings → Options → RESTORE PURCHASES.
  3. Wait a moment for confirmation that your unlocked items have been restored.

2. How do I back up or sync my coins, ELO rating, and progress across devices?

Open Settings → Options (or tap your Duelist Profile badge at the top of the Main Menu) and select Sign in with Apple or Sign in with Google. Once linked, your coins, ELO rating, campaign unlocks, and match statistics automatically sync to the cloud.

3. How do I permanently delete my account and cloud data?

You have full control over your account and data directly inside the app without needing to contact customer service:

  1. Open Dala and go to Settings → Options (under Cloud Identity & Sync) or open your Duelist Profile from the Main Menu.
  2. Tap the crimson DELETE ACCOUNT button.
  3. Review the confirmation dialog and tap DELETE ACCOUNT to confirm.

This permanently deletes your cloud player account, Cloud Save backup, linked Sign in with Apple / Google credentials, ELO rating, statistics, and local profile data immediately.

4. How does "Play With Friend" private multiplayer work?

From the Main Menu, tap Play → Play Online → Play With Friend. One player taps Create Game to generate a room code and shares that code with their friend, who taps Join Game and enters the code to start a live 1v1 duel.

5. What should I include if I email Support about a technical issue?

To help us resolve your issue as quickly as possible, please include:

  • Your device model (e.g., iPhone 15, iPad Air) and OS version.
  • Your in-game Duelist Name (if applicable).
  • A brief description or screenshot of what happened.
